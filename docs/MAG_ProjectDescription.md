## SHORT (for a project description field, ~110 words)

Maine Academy of Gymnastics (MAG) is a family-run gymnastics training center in
Westbrook, Maine, just outside Portland, operating since 1991 and owned by the
Amundson family. It's a USA Gymnastics member club in a 13,500 sq ft facility
split into a main gym with full USAG apparatus, trampolines and an in-ground
foam pit, plus a separate two-level "Jungle Gym" sized for ages 1–6.

Revenue comes from monthly recreational tuition (year-round continuous
enrollment, $45 annual membership fee for new students), preschool programs,
invite-only Pre-Team and the competitive American Flyers boys' and girls' teams,
prepaid Open Gym sessions, and the annually hosted American Flyers Cup meet.
Registration, billing and skill tracking run on Jackrabbit Class.

## LONG (fuller project context — better if the field allows it)

### What the business is

Maine Academy of Gymnastics (MAG) — 20 Terminal Street, Westbrook, ME 04092.
(207) 856-0232 · info@maineacademy.com · maineacademy.com

Family-owned and operated since 1991 by the Amundsons. A USA Gymnastics member
club serving the greater Portland area, with students traveling from across the
state. Tagline: "Where Fitness Is Fun."

### Facility

13,500 sq ft, described by the business as one of the largest and best-equipped
training facilities in New England. Two distinct spaces:

- **Main gym** — full USAG apparatus for boys (6 events) and girls (4 events),
  trampolines, large in-ground foam pit, rock climbing wall.
- **The Jungle Gym** — a separate two-level preschool space with "Just My Size"
  modified equipment, slides, trampolines, and two foam pits, designed for ages 1–6.

Three parent viewing rooms with WiFi throughout.

### Revenue lines

1. **Recreational tuition** — billed monthly, year-round, continuous curriculum
   with no session start or end point. Families may withdraw, re-enroll, or switch
   days and times at any point subject to availability. A $45 annual membership
   fee applies to all new students. Classes require a minimum of three students to run.
2. **Preschool / the Jungle** — the primary funnel feeder; the business states it
   specializes in preschool development. First Flight (walking–3 yrs, adult-assisted
   1:1), Second Flight (3–4 yrs, 5:1 ratio), Soaring Eagles (4–5½ yrs). Super Flyers 1
   and 2 exist in the structure but currently show no available classes.
3. **Competitive teams** — invite-only Pre-Team, then the American Flyers boys' and
   girls' teams. Maine State Champions for over a decade; athletes compete at State,
   Regional and National USAG levels. Higher training hours, higher revenue per
   athlete, substantially higher family commitment.
4. **Open Gym** — one-hour supervised sessions, prepaid and preregistered at least
   one hour in advance. Drop-ins have been discontinued. Open to participants up to
   age 21. No charge or registration for non-participating parents and siblings.
5. **Hosted meets** — MAG runs the American Flyers Cup annually, drawing gymnasts
   from across New England. Separate website and economics.

### The progression ladder (core retention mechanic)

Girls' recreational: Red → White → Blue → Silver → Gold Wings, with weekly hours
rising from 1¼ to 2 as students advance. Boys' recreational: Red → White → Blue.
Each advancement gates on a coach-verified skill checklist that parents can view
in the Jackrabbit portal. Tumbling (ages 5–21; beginner, intermediate, advanced)
runs alongside as a non-Olympic-track option. Red Wings entry requires age 5 by
October 15 of the current year.

### Operations

- **Jackrabbit Class** is the system of record: registration, class availability,
  monthly billing, and student skill checklists exposed to parents via a portal.
- Coaching staff are USAG and/or Red Cross safety certified; senior staff attend
  national clinics and training seminars annually.
- **American Flyers Booster Club** — a separate parent-run 501(c)(3) whose primary
  function is fundraising for the competitive teams.
- Website is WordPress. Active on Instagram and Facebook.

### Known issues and constraints (as of July 2026)

- **Staffing is the visible bottleneck.** Coach hiring occupies a homepage slide, a
  ticker item, and a floating site-wide button, and offers tuition discounts as a
  perk. A "Coach in Training" program grows talent internally rather than hiring it.
- **Stale COVID-19 notices** remain on every girls' and boys' recreational class
  block: "Due to COVID-19, Gymnastics Classes are under a revised schedule and
  format until September." The page was last modified February 2026.
- **Super Flyers 1 and 2** both display "no classes available at this time."
- **No pricing published anywhere on the site.** Every path routes to Jackrabbit or
